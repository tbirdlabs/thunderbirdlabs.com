Calliope

In progress

An NFC-triggered media player. It’s split between an ESP32 and a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W that talk to each other over MQTT, and the sound goes out through interchangeable output modules (I’m calling them sinks).

Files and build instructions will show up here when it’s done, and not before.
